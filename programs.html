What we do

Eight kinds of care, working together.

No single program raises a child. We run these side by side at Wema Center, from the day a child arrives to years after they leave.

Child safeguarding

Every staff member and long-term volunteer passes a police clearance and a safeguarding interview before ever meeting a child. We follow Kenya's National Child Protection standards, run yearly refresher training, and every home has a confidential reporting line children are taught to use from day one.
