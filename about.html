About us

Founded on one street, in one storm.

Wema Center began in 2015 when Everlyn Chebet, then a Mombasa primary school teacher, took in three siblings she found sleeping outside her classroom during the long rains. She fostered them in her own house for eight months while registering Wema Center as a charitable trust. Eleven years later, we run a home for twenty children in Mombasa, built around one idea: a child does better with a bed of their own, a school uniform that fits, and adults who stay.

We are governed by a seven-person board that meets quarterly, audited annually by an independent firm, and registered with the Kenya NGO Coordination Board. The home has house parents, a resident social worker and a nurse, and a direct link to the nearest public school and government health facility in Mombasa County. We work toward safe family reunification wherever possible, and toward independence — a trade, a place to live, a plan — for the young people who age out of our care at 18.
